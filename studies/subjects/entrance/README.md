# ENTR — Entrance-exam preparation

Subject ID: `ENTR`. Opened 2026-10-20 at the owner's request.

Goal: pace and accuracy for a college aptitude test and an engineering entrance exam.
Exam names, schools and dates are kept out of this repository by the owner's rule.

## Track: SAT-style Reading & Writing (helper mode)

- Teacher: the Khan Academy SAT Reading and Writing course. Claude is a helper, not a mentor.
- Claude answers questions, explains why an answer is right or wrong, points to the
  Khan unit, and builds timed drills only when asked. Questions Claude writes are labelled
  "SAT-style practice", never official items.
- Pacing target: about 70 s per question, tightened once accuracy is stable.
- A Khan unit is done after two clean timed passes in separate sessions.
- One timed mixed set per week.

Progress lives in [STATE.md](STATE.md). A Math track may be added to this subject later.
