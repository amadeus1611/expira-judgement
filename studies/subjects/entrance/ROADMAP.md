# ENTRANCE: roadmap

Proposed 2026-10-20. The aim is speed and accuracy under time, not new math: the owner reports being
comfortable with the math content (self-report, not yet assessed here).

## Phase 1: SAT Math and SAT Reading and Writing (primary)

Source: Khan Academy SAT courses (owner's choice).

1. Timed diagnostic: one mixed math set and one reading and writing set, to place the start.
2. Per Khan unit: work the unit, then a timed drill in the drill artifact. A unit counts
   as done after two clean timed passes in separate sessions (same rule as Greek review).
3. Weekly: one timed mixed set; track accuracy and seconds per question.

## Phase 2: gaps the engineering exam adds

Reported coverage of the engineering exam (official 2026 announcement): algebra, calculus,
geometry, trigonometry, physics, chemistry. Phase 1 covers algebra, geometry and some
trigonometry. Remaining:
