# ENTRANCE: roadmap

Proposed 2026-10-20, widened the same day at the owner's request: prepare for everything
both exams could plausibly cover, deep enough to make first-year engineering easier. The aim
for the exams is speed and accuracy under time; the owner reports being comfortable with the
math content (self-report, not yet assessed here).

## Two kinds of subject

- **SAT (Khan Academy)**: Khan teaches. Claude answers questions, explains mistakes and
  records timed logs. No mentoring. Tracked here in `ENTRANCE`.
- **Book subjects**: mentored like the Greek track. Claude teaches from the owner's
  downloaded book, checks lesson claims with ej-study, drills in an interactive artifact
  and adapts to the results. Each one is its own subject (IDs below), so it can carry on
  into first year.

## Phase 1: SAT Math and SAT Reading and Writing (now, daily)

1. Timed diagnostic: one mixed math set, then one R&W set.
2. Per Khan unit: a timed drill. Pacing targets ~95 s per math question and ~70 s per R&W
   question, tightened once accurate. A unit is done after two clean timed passes in
   separate sessions.
3. Weekly: one timed mixed set; track accuracy and seconds per question.

## Phase 2: book subjects (in this order, overlapping)

The engineering exam's reported 2026 coverage was algebra, calculus, geometry, trigonometry,
physics and chemistry. The aptitude test's sections are unpublished; reasoning and
general-science parts are assumptions. `BIO` is dropped if the test has no science part.

## Phase 3: timed mixed practice and mock exams for both exams.

## Study method (all subjects)

Retrieval warm-up first; spaced review at about 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days, adjusted to the
results; interleaving once topics have passed on their own; accuracy before speed; done
means two clean cold passes in separate sessions. Recovery: at most two ~45-minute blocks
per subject per day with a break, one light review-only day a week, protect sleep, and stop
a drill when accuracy drops from tiredness. These session lengths are a chosen starting
point, not a measured value for this learner.
