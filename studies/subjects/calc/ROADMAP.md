# CALC: roadmap

Proposed 2026-10-20. Calendar placement follows the `ENTRANCE` plan; placement results
set the real pace.

## Before the exams: Volume 1, ch. 1-3 (functions, limits, derivatives)

1. Placement diagnostic per chapter, written from the book once its sections are provided.
   Sections shown as known are marked `skipped (placement)`, not `done`.
2. Remaining sections in book order: cold warm-up, short re-teach with citations,
   `ej-study` check of the lesson claims, then a timed drill artifact.

## After the exams: Volume 1, ch. 4-6 (applications of derivatives, integration,
applications of integration), then Volume 2.

## Method

- Each session: 2-3 cold warm-up questions from older material, then re-teach, then drill
  (timer and per-question seconds log; the owner pastes the log back).
- Accuracy first (untimed), then timed, then tightened.
- Done = two clean cold passes in separate sessions.
- Spaced review at about 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days: longer after a clean pass, shorter
  after a miss. Interleave once topics have passed on their own.
- At most two ~45-minute blocks per day with a real break; stop a drill when accuracy
  drops from tiredness. These lengths are a chosen starting point, not a measured value.
- Math validation: derivations and independent calculations, not only source support.
