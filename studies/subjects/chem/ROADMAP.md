# CHEM — Roadmap

Proposed 2026-10-20. The calendar is a proposal; placement results set the real pace.

## Before the exams (priority topics)

1. Placement diagnostic across the priority topics, written from the book once the
   owner supplies it. Topics shown as known are skipped; the rest are taught.
2. Priority topics, in book order:
   - measurement, units and significant figures
   - atoms, molecules, ions and the periodic table
   - chemical bonding
   - the mole and stoichiometry (composition, equations, reaction yields)
   - reaction types
   - gases
   - thermochemistry
   - solutions
3. Chapter numbers and section titles are filled into [TRACKER.md](TRACKER.md) from
   the downloaded book's table of contents, not from memory.

## After the exams

The rest of the book, for first-year engineering chemistry, plus any gaps from the logs.

## Method

- Each session: a 2–3 question cold warm-up from older material, a short re-teach from
  the book, then an interactive HTML drill artifact. The owner pastes the results log back.
- Stoichiometry and unit problems: untimed until accurate, then timed, then tightened.
- A section is done after two clean cold passes in separate sessions.
- Spaced review at about 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days, longer after a correct answer and shorter
  after a miss. Mixed sets once each topic has passed on its own.
- At most two ~45-minute blocks a day with a real break. Stop a drill when accuracy drops
  from tiredness. These lengths are a chosen starting point, not a measured value.
