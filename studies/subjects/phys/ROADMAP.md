# PHYS — Proposed sequence

Proposal (2026-10-20). The placement diagnostic decides which sections are skipped.
Chapter and section numbers are from the inspected tables of contents.

## Before the entrance exam

| Step | Book scope | Topic group |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Vol. 1 ch. 1–17 + Vol. 2 ch. 1 | Placement diagnostic, one short group per row below |
| 1 | Vol. 1 ch. 1–2 | Units, measurement, significant figures, vectors |
| 2 | Vol. 1 ch. 3–4 | Kinematics in one and two dimensions, projectiles, circular motion |
| 3 | Vol. 1 ch. 5–6 | Newton's laws, free-body diagrams, friction, centripetal force |
| 4 | Vol. 1 ch. 7–8 | Work, kinetic energy, power, potential energy, conservation of energy |
| 5 | Vol. 1 ch. 9 | Momentum, impulse, collisions, centre of mass |
| 6 | Vol. 1 ch. 10–12 | Rotation, torque, angular momentum, static equilibrium |
| 7 | Vol. 1 ch. 13 | Gravitation |
| 8 | Vol. 1 ch. 14 | Fluids |
| 9 | Vol. 1 ch. 15–17 | Oscillations, waves, sound |
| 10 | Vol. 2 ch. 1 | Temperature and heat |
| — | Mixed | Interleaved timed sets and spaced review of passed sections |

Sections that need calculus are deferred until the matching calculus is covered,
unless an algebra form is enough. Candidates by title (confirm from the text):
Vol. 1 3.6 and 10.5.

## After the exam (first-year EE)

- Vol. 1 sections deferred above; Vol. 2 ch. 2–4 (kinetic theory, laws of thermodynamics).
- Vol. 2 ch. 5–16: electric charges and fields, Gauss's law, potential, capacitance,
  current and resistance, DC circuits, magnetism, induction, inductance, AC circuits,
  electromagnetic waves.

No step is completed.
