covers physics, then carry the subject into first-year electrical engineering.

- Source: OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* now; *Volume 2* (thermodynamics,

  derivation, units and an independent computation ([procedures](../../PROCEDURES.md)).

  (not yet opened); otherwise use the algebra form the book also gives.
- EE link: this subject is a prerequisite record for [EE](../electrical-engineering/README.md).
  EE links here; progress is kept only in this folder.

Resume from [STATE.md](STATE.md), then [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) and
[TRACKER.md](TRACKER.md) (progress by book section).

## Session method

1. Cold warm-up: 2–3 questions from older passed or due material, before anything new.
2. Short re-teach from the book section, with citations.
3. Interactive HTML drill artifact: concept questions and problems, with a timer and a
   per-question seconds log. The owner pastes the log back; record it as given.
4. Accuracy first (untimed), then timed, then tighter time targets.

- A section is done after two clean cold passes in separate sessions.
- Spaced review at about 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days, lengthened after a clean review and
  shortened after a miss. Interleave topics once each has passed on its own.
- At most two ~45-minute blocks per day with a real break between them. Stop a drill
  when accuracy drops from fatigue.
- Drill items written by the tutor are practice items, not official exam items.

These are the owner's chosen study rules. The session lengths are a plan setting, not
a measured value for this learner.
