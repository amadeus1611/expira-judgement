# SECPLUS — Proposed preparation sequence

1. Build shared computing/networking foundations through the `FOUND` track.
2. Confirm the intended exam version and obtain its official objective document.
3. Map sourced lessons and relevant labs to those objectives: security concepts,
   threats, identity, cryptography, architecture, operations and risk as applicable.
4. Build original practice questions with reviewed stems, assumptions, supported
   keys, explanations and reasoning for every distractor. Keep unresolved items unscored.
5. Use reviewed scenario questions and timed practice; record actual responses,
   coverage gaps and repeated misconceptions. Review weak areas with varied examples.
6. Assess readiness from documented coverage and performance on unfamiliar, reviewed
   material. Do not invent a pass probability or imply practice is an official exam.

No stage is completed. Use [procedures](../../PROCEDURES.md), preserving source,
question and result versions. The future AI-assisted system has a separate
[deployment evaluation](../../LABS.md); passing an exam does not establish that readiness.
