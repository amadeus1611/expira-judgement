mascfemneut
nom sgὁ λόγοςἡ γραφή · ἡ ὥρατὸ ἔργον
acc sgτὸν λόγοντὴν γραφήν · τὴν ὥραντὸ ἔργον
nom plοἱ λόγοιαἱ γραφαί · αἱ ὧραιτὰ ἔργα
acc plτοὺς λόγουςτὰς γραφάς · τὰς ὥραςτὰ ἔργα

3. Patterns that make it stick (Mounce's hints, §6.14, §6.23)

  • ν = accusative singular in every gender. See ν alone at the end: think "object".
  • vowel + ι = nominative plural: οι is masculine, αι is feminine.
  • vowel + ς = accusative plural: ους is masculine, ας is feminine.
  • Neuter: nom = acc (rule 2), and the plural ends in α (rule 3). Context decides subject or object.
  • The article = rough breathing or τ, then the vowel (ο for masc/neut, η/α for fem), then the ending. Only τό breaks the pattern.
  • The feminine article always has η in the singular, even on an α noun: ἡ ὥρα, τὴν ὥραν.
  • Feminine η nouns switch to α in the plural: γραφή → γραφαί (§6.16).
  • Find the subject by the ending, not the position: ψυχὰς σώσει θεός = God will save souls.
