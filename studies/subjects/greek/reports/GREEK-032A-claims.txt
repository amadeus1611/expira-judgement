The Greek infinitive is a verbal noun.
The infinitive has no person and no number!
The present active infinitive ending is ειν.
The present active infinitive of λύω is λύειν.
The present ("continuous") infinitive is built on the present tense stem.
The infinitive built on the present stem indicates a continuous action.
The infinitive has no time significance, only aspect: continuous; undefined; perfected.
ειν is actually a contraction of εεν.
βλέπειν means "to continue to see."
An infinitive is most easily recognized as a verb preceded by the word "to."
The infinitive tells you who is doing the action.
