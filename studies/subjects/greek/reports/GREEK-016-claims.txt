The present active tense uses the primary active endings: ω, εις, ει, ομεν, ετε, ουσι(ν).
The real personal endings are -, ς, ι, μεν, τε, νσι.
The present tense verb is composed of three parts: present verbal stem, connecting vowel, and primary personal ending.
In the indicative mood, if the personal ending begins with mu or nu, the connecting vowel is omicron.
Otherwise the connecting vowel is epsilon.
A verb must agree with its subject in person and number.
A movable nu can be added to the third person plural personal ending.
If the subject is "we" the verb would end in ομεν.
λύετε means you are loosing.
The ending ετε is first person plural.
