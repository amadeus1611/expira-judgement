# Study-work instructions

Read `README.md` and its resume files before teaching, making study material or
building practice questions. Follow the owner's current instructions over older
repository plans. This workflow is for sourced learning and exam preparation.

- Use `SUBJECTS.md` labels and subject folders. Read the active subject's STATE
  and roadmap; keep shared foundations in one canonical record, linked by other tracks.
- Use discipline-appropriate validation from `PROCEDURES.md`; evidence verdicts
  alone do not prove mathematical correctness, working code or experimental results.
- Preserve the beginner starting point until an assessment supports a change.
- Explain from first principles, connect concepts to demonstrations, and check
  understanding with unfamiliar applications. Full-time availability has been
  stated; an exact daily schedule and exam date have not.
- Teach code and debugging explicitly. Build sandbox labs when prerequisites are
  established, following `PROCEDURES.md`; record the actual artifacts and observations.
- Follow `LABS.md` for the longer-term AI-assisted security-system goal. Production
  readiness needs separate evaluation; do not promise it follows from exam preparation.
- Cite independent sources with version/section and actual access date. Record
  source authority, licence inspection and EXPIRA outcome as separate facts.
- Never invent learner results, scores, mastery, source inspections or verdicts.
- Label assumptions, conjectures, analogies and established source-supported facts
  distinctly. Use simplified models to teach, stating their limits; do not treat an
  engine `unsure` as permission to invent a factual explanation.
- Save substantive study exchanges promptly and follow the owner's standing merge
  preference in `PROCEDURES.md`, retaining required validation and access checks.
- Validate stems, answer keys, explanations and distractor reasoning before using
  questions in scored practice. Label ambiguity and unresolved evidence explicitly.
- Update the affected subject's STATE, hub `STATE.md` and `SESSION_LOG.md` before finishing a study session.
  Record what happened, what remains open and the concrete next action.
- Keep `HANDOFF.md` current enough for Claude or Codex/GPT to resume without assuming
  mastery or reconstructing decisions from chat history alone.
- Keep material updates traceable. Recheck affected claims and questions when
  their sources or explanations change; preserve the versions used for past results.
- Do not modify or propose changes to `ej-evidence`, judge/threshold code,
  `ej-bench/src/standard.rs`, models, calibration data or existing decision tests
  as part of study work. Decision tuning is Claude's exclusive responsibility.

Do not redirect a study request into the historical decision-engine improvement
roadmap. Continue the study plan in `STATE.md` unless the owner changes the goal.
